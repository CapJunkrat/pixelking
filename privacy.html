PixelKing Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

PixelKing ("the game") is a single-player game developed by CapJunkrat. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it on Android, in a web browser, or on any other platform.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs, and the Android app requests no permissions (including no internet access).

Data stored on your device

Your settings, current run, achievements and progress are saved only on your own device (in the app's private storage, or in your browser's storage for the web version). This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the browser's site data for the web version, deletes it.

Battle codes

The game can copy a "battle code" to your clipboard so you can report a bug. It contains only game data about that battle (the game version, random seed, board, pieces, items and moves), no personal information, and it is shared only if you choose to paste it somewhere yourself.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/CapJunkrat/pixelking/issues.

PixelKing 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 20 日

PixelKing（以下简称"本游戏"）是由 CapJunkrat 开发的单人游戏。本政策说明你在安卓、浏览器或其他平台上游玩时，你的信息会如何处理。

我们收集的信息

没有。本游戏不收集、不传输、不出售、也不分享任何个人信息。游戏没有账号系统、没有广告、没有数据统计、没有崩溃上报，也没有接入任何第三方 SDK。安卓版不申请任何权限（包括联网权限）。

保存在你设备上的数据

你的设置、当前冒险进度、成就等数据只保存在你自己的设备上（安卓版保存在应用的私有存储中，网页版保存在浏览器存储中），不会离开你的设备。卸载应用或清除网页版的网站数据，就会删除这些数据。

对局码

游戏可以把"对局码"复制到剪贴板，方便你反馈问题。对局码只包含那场战斗的游戏数据（游戏版本、随机种子、棋盘、棋子、道具和走法），不含个人信息，只有你自己把它粘贴出去时才会被分享。

儿童

本游戏不会有意收集任何人的信息，包括 13 岁以下的儿童。

政策变更

如果本政策有变化，会在本页面发布新版本并更新日期。

联系方式

对本政策有疑问，请在 github.com/CapJunkrat/pixelking/issues 提交 Issue。
