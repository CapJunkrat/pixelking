# PixelKing 0.2.3

- Catapult: no longer breaks rocks; its shots fly over them instead ("Over rocks" trait).
  Blast Catapult and Sky Archer still break rocks.
- Shadow King: blinks and strikes anywhere in the 7x7 area around it (48 squares), not only along
  the eight lines.
- War King and Shield King: up to 2 squares straight and 1 diagonally (pieces in between still block).
- Rarity: King is Legendary; War, Shadow and Shield King are Mythic.
- Codex: sorted from the rarest down (then by worth).
- Piece styles: only the carved figurines remain; the style picker in Settings hides itself while
  there is only one style and comes back when a second one is added.
- Piece value is no longer shown anywhere (opening draft, codex, battle long-press, roster,
  pre-battle, fusion preview). It is still used behind the scenes for enemy budgets, AI and sorting.
  The opening draft cards show one-word traits in its place.
